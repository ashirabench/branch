# Summaries before the priors were widened (13 November 2026)

These are the benchmark and validation summaries with the national priors, before two priors were widened to include published values: the heat for washing (upper bound 0.4 to 0.5 MJ) and the mass of the 1 L refillable glass bottle in Brazil (upper bound 750 to 950 g). They allow the comparison of the results before and after the widening.
