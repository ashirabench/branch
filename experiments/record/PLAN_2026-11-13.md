# Plan fixed before running: other impact categories and knowledge ablations

Written on 13 November 2026, before any of these runs existed. The two parts answer commitments
of the proposal that the paper listed as missing. Nothing below is chosen after looking at results.

## What stays the same in every run

- The 18 benchmark cases (four packages, pairs, in PT, DE and BR) and `data/regions/` as on this date.
- The chosen setting of the paper: tau = 2.5%, three questions per round, N = 20,000 samples.
- Seeds 0 to 29, simulated experts from the priors and from their 10% tails.
- Four strategies: `decision_flip`, `sobol`, `entropy`, `random`.
- Two outcomes: mean questions until the certificate, and the share of certified verdicts that are wrong.
- The results are reported as descriptive. No new p values: the cases are the same 18, so further
  tests would only add comparisons.

## Part 1. Other impact categories

The verdict is settled for `Acidification`, `Water use` and `Resource use fossils` instead of
`Climate change`. These three were named before any run. They share the cases and the choices of the
climate runs, so they are not independent evidence. For each category the report also gives the number
of cases certified from the priors alone, because such a case tests nothing.

Check of the new code path: the option `--category "Climate change"` must reproduce the summaries in
`paper/data/` for the four strategies.

## Part 2. Ablations of the knowledge sources (climate change)

The simulated expert and the reference verdict always come from the national model of the paper.
Only the agent's beliefs change.

- `no_location`: the agent uses the global electricity mix of the database (`electricity_mv_GLO`)
  and the default priors of `data/regions/GLO.json` and `branch/catalogue.py`. The expert answers
  with national values, and a certificate counts as wrong when it disagrees with the sign of the
  national model at the expert's world. National values can fall outside the default ranges; this
  mismatch is what the ablation measures.
- `uniform`: the agent keeps the national electricity mix and the national ranges, but every
  continuous prior becomes uniform between its lowest and highest possible value and every
  categorical prior gives equal probability to its options.
- The ablation of pedigree spreads named in the proposal cannot be run, because BRANCH never
  implemented them.

Check of the new code path (placebo): `--ablation none` must reproduce the summaries in `paper/data/`.
