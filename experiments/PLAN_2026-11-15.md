# Plan of version 0.2, fixed before any run

Written on 15 November 2026, before the first run of version 0.2. The delivery of
27 November 2026 is version 0.1.1 and is not touched by anything below.

## Rules

1. Every experiment below is specified here before it runs: setting, seeds, metric and what
   counts as which answer. A change after seeing results is written in this file with its date
   and reason, and the earlier result is kept.
2. Changes that alter the existing numbers (block A) enter together and the benchmark runs once.
3. Results are reported whether or not they favour BRANCH.

## B1. The certificate in the acidification category (runs first; needs no openLCA)

**What is known.** In version 0.1.1, with experts drawn from the tails of the priors, 2.6% of the
certified verdicts for Acidification were wrong, with an interval over cases of 0.2% to 5.4%,
against a target of 2.5%. The interval contains the target, so the 30 seeds do not show whether
the certificate misses its target in this category.

**What the certificate promises.** At most tau wrong certified verdicts when the true values
come from the priors. Experts from the tails break that assumption on purpose, so an error above
tau with them is a property of the stress test and not a defect of the code. The question is how
large the error is, not whether a guarantee is violated.

**Setting.** As in the paper: strategy decision flip, three questions per round, N = 20,000,
tau = 2.5%, the 18 cases (four packages, PT, DE, BR), experts from the tails.

**Seeds.** 2000 to 2299, new (the paper used 0 to 29 and 1000 to 1029): 300 sessions per case,
5,400 sessions per category.

**Categories.** Acidification (the question) and Climate change (the reference, same seeds).

**Metric.** Share of certified verdicts that are wrong, with the 95% percentile interval of a
bootstrap over the 18 cases (the interval of the paper) and the exact binomial interval over
sessions for reference. Also the same share case by case.

**Reading, fixed now.**
- Interval entirely above 2.5%: with tail experts the certificate errs above its target in this
  category. Then, and only then, a look case by case at where the wrong verdicts are.
- Interval entirely at or below 2.5%: the 2.6% of version 0.1.1 was sampling noise.
- Interval containing 2.5%: still undecided at 300 seeds; reported as such, no further seeds.

## Block A. Changes that alter the numbers of the paper (they enter together, one benchmark run)

**A2. Circular Footprint Formula with the energy credit (code and data since 16 November 2026).**
Parameters copied from Part C of Annex II, version 2.1 (`data/cff/`, with what comes from where). The
energy term uses B = 0 and the electricity and steam that the ELCD waste-to-energy plant delivers per kg.
Reading fixed now: the paper keeps cut-off as the main model, because BRANCH states ILCD Situation A.
The formula is a second system model, reported as a sensitivity: for each of the 18 cases, the sign of
the central verdict and the mean number of questions under both models (decision flip, three questions
per round, tau = 2.5%, seeds 0 to 29, experts from the priors). No claim that one model is right. A
case whose verdict differs between the two models is named.

**A3. Uncertainty of the datasets (code since 16 November 2026; needs a simulation in openLCA).**
BAFU states a lognormal uncertainty for many of its exchanges. `experiments/extract_uncertainty.py` runs
200 Monte Carlo runs of each dataset of the 18 cases in openLCA and stores the geometric standard
deviation of each impact category. With `--inventory-uncertainty on`, every factor varies in the
draws of BRANCH by that spread: one draw per dataset, shared by the two products when both use it,
never asked, and part of the probability that the verdict is wrong.
- Status: a robustness analysis, not the main model. Two reasons. The factors are simulated one at a
  time, so two datasets that share upstream processes (the same electricity inside PET and inside
  glass) are treated as independent, which is an approximation. And it is off by default in the code
  until this run has been read.
- Setting: the 18 cases, the chosen setting, four strategies, experts from the priors and from the
  tails. A tail expert is wrong about the choices, not about the database: its database draws come
  from their distribution.
- Metrics: share of sessions that reach the certificate (it can now fall below 100%, because nobody
  can answer the database's uncertainty), mean questions, share of certified verdicts that are wrong
  with its interval over cases; per case, the share of sessions certified.
- Reading, fixed now: the two statements are read as in B3. A case in which fewer than half of the
  sessions reach the certificate is named "not decidable within the uncertainty of the database".
  No statement of the main results is changed by this run; it is reported next to them.
- Checks: the refactoring that made room for it changes nothing on a factor file without uncertainty
  (same runs before and after, identical except the timing columns, 16 November 2026). Factors whose
  runs have a median more than 10% away from the value BRANCH uses, or no usable spread, are listed.

**B2. Other product families (fixed on 16 November 2026, before any session ran).**

**Order of the runs.** The main benchmark again with the priors of A1 (`bash reproduce.sh experiments`),

runs; that output is not a result and is not reported.

## B3. A second database, ELCD 3.2 (fixed on 15 November 2026, before any run)

**Question.** Do two statements of the paper survive when the unit factors come from another database?
1. Decision flip reaches the certificate with fewer questions than entropy.
2. With experts drawn from the tails of the priors, the share of certified verdicts that are wrong
   stays near the target of 2.5%.

**What the test does not answer.** Whether the verdicts are current. ELCD was discontinued in 2018
and its data sets state validity periods that end between 2009 and 2015.

**Factors.** A hybrid file. The 12 cases need 29 factors: 21 come from ELCD and 8 stay from BAFU:2026,
because ELCD has no separate secondary aluminium, no disposal of aluminium and no plastic conversion.
The table is `data/second_database/elcd32_map.json` and does not change after results are seen.
`experiments/second_database/read_elcd.py` computes the ELCD factors from the database file, outside
openLCA, with the method stored in that file (EF 3.0 Method (adapted)); `experiments/make_hybrid_factors.py`
writes the hybrid. The incineration data sets used are the ones without an energy credit.

**Cases.** 12: the six pairs of the four packages, in Portugal and Germany. Brazil is left out because
ELCD has no Brazilian data.

**Model and priors.** Identical to those of the BAFU run it is compared with. The test runs together
with the single run of block A, so that both sides use the same model.

**Setting.** As in the paper: tau = 2.5%, three questions per round, N = 20,000, seeds 0 to 29, four
strategies (`decision_flip`, `sobol`, `entropy`, `random`), experts from the priors and from the tails.

**Categories.** Climate change is the question. Acidification is reported as description.

**Metrics.**
- Mean questions until the certificate, by strategy.
- Difference decision flip minus entropy, with the 95% percentile interval of a bootstrap over the 12 cases.
- Share of certified verdicts that are wrong with tail experts, with the same kind of interval.
- Description, case by case: the sign of the central verdict with BAFU and with the hybrid, and the
  number of cases certified from the priors alone.
- The comparison with BAFU uses the same 12 cases, not the 18.

**Reading, fixed now.**
- Statement 1 holds if the interval of the difference lies entirely below zero. If it contains zero:
  "not shown with 12 cases". If it lies entirely above zero, the statement fails with this database.
- Statement 2: interval entirely above 2.5%, the certificate errs above its target with this database;
  interval containing 2.5%, undecided; interval entirely at or below 2.5%, the target holds.
- A verdict that changes between the databases is reported case by case and counts neither as a
  failure nor as a success of BRANCH: it measures the databases. Large differences are known before the
  run (electricity of Portugal 0.78 against 0.19 kg CO2 eq per kWh, lorry 0.067 against 0.22 per t km).
- No new p values, no further seeds after the results are seen.

**Checks of the code, before the test.**
1. Placebo: the hybrid with nothing replaced must reproduce the BAFU run. Done on 15 November 2026 on
   one case (PET against refillable glass, Portugal, three seeds, four strategies, tail experts):
   every column equal except the three timing columns. It runs again on the 12 cases with the test.
2. My calculation against openLCA: three ELCD processes (PET bottle grade, electricity of Portugal,
   incineration of PET) computed in openLCA must agree with `elcd32_factors.json` within 1e-6 relative.
   This needs ELCD imported in openLCA. If the check is not made, the paper says so.

**Added on 15 November 2026, before any run.** An audit of the calculation showed that methane weighs
36.8 in EF 3.0 and 27.9 in IPCC 2021. So the sign of the central verdict of each case is also reported
with the ELCD factors computed by IPCC 2021 GWP 100 (`--climate ipcc2021`). Description only: it does
not enter the reading of the two statements.

**What can invalidate the reading.** The method in the ELCD file is EF 3.0 and the BAFU factors use
EF 3.1. The hybrid mixes the two in the 8 factors that stay from BAFU, and the paper declares it.

**Record of check 2 (16 November 2026).** Made on all 21 processes instead of three, in openLCA 2.6.2
with the ELCD file restored and updated by openLCA.
- First attempt: 4 of 21 agreed and 17 did not. The error was in the check, not in the factors: it used
  the BRANCH function written for BAFU, which lets openLCA link every leftover waste flow of a process
  to any process that has that flow. For the electricity of Portugal openLCA sent 0.00181 kg of spoil to
  the container glass data set, as if making glass treated spoil: 0.00181 / 0.00200 kg per kg of glass
  x 0.80 kg CO2 eq is the 0.72 too much that it showed (1.51 against 0.78).
- Second attempt, linking only the default providers (in ELCD, empty "Dummy_" processes): the 21
  factors agree in Climate change and in Acidification, largest relative difference 3.7e-16.
- Consequence for BRANCH itself: `branch/olca_extract.py` as released in 0.1.2 would return wrong
  factors for a database of aggregated processes such as ELCD. It was validated on BAFU only, which
  holds unit processes. Version 0.2 has to choose the linking by the type of the process and say so.

To repeat (after block A): `python experiments/make_hybrid_factors.py` and then
`BRANCH_FACTORS=data/factors_second/hybrid_elcd32.json ONLY="PT DE" SUFFIX=_elcd TRUTH=tails TAU=0.025 bash experiments/run_benchmark.sh`.

  against the aluminium can in the three regions, cases that the agent certifies without a question.
